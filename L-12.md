1. **What is Diligence**?
**AI Fluency** = working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely.
**The other three Ds (Delegation, Description, Discernment) mostly address effectiveness and efficiency.**

**Diligence** focuses mostly on the ethical and safety side, which is just as crucial.
At its heart, Diligence = **taking responsibility for my AI interactions, so my AI use is productive and also rigorous, transparent, and accountable**.

The lesson's analogy: d**riving a car. We don't only care about getting from A to B quickly. We also consider safety, follow traffic rules, and stay aware of how our driving affects others.**

**Bigger questions Diligence asks**:

What are the implications of working with this AI?
Who might be affected by what is created, or by any missed inaccuracies?
Who has access to the data used to produce this output?
How do I make sure my interaction and its outcome match ethical standards and values?

AI systems and our use of them don't exist in a vacuum. Responsible use needs awareness of broader context.

3. **Key points**
**Transparency** is not only about rules. It is about maintaining trust and respect in relationships. People have the right to know when AI played a significant role in content or in decisions that affect them.
Different settings (personal, academic, creative, professional) have different expectations about disclosing AI use. The responsibility is on me to understand and meet them.
When I share AI-generated content, I (not the AI) am ultimately responsible for its accuracy and appropriateness.

Diligence isn't always straightforward, because contexts and stakeholders have different standards. So:
Develop personal guidelines for AI use that match my ethics and values.
In professional settings, learn organizational policies and industry standards.
Legal and regulatory frameworks around AI are still emerging and will keep evolving. Staying informed is part of Diligence.
All three types work together to form the complete Diligence competency.

5. **Closing message of the lesson**

Diligence reminds us that working with AI comes with responsibilities:

Be thoughtful about the systems we choose and how we use them.
Be honest about AI's role in our work.
Be accountable for what we create with AI.

We all want AI that is fair, safe, and beneficial to society, and our own behavior plays a key role in making that happen.

5.  **Quick revision**
Diligence = the ethical and safe side of AI use: responsible, transparent, accountable.
Creation: choose systems and data carefully (privacy, security, policies).
Transparency: be honest about AI's role with those who need to know.
Deployment: verify and take responsibility for what I share. I'm accountable, not the AI.
Build personal guidelines, learn organizational policies, and stay informed because AI laws keep changing.

**Link to AI Security (my goal)**
Creation Diligence is the foundation of security habits: data privacy, access, and vendor/tool choice (who owns and can access the data I enter).
Transparency Diligence supports trust, auditing, and incident response (knowing where AI was involved).
Deployment Diligence is a safeguard against hallucinated or manipulated output reaching real people.
Emerging regulation is a real part of the AI security and governance field. Staying informed is part of the job.
