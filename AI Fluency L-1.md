# AI-Fluency

The big idea

Most people learn AI through tips and tricks like “use this magic prompt.” Those go out of date quickly. This course teaches a lasting way of thinking about working with AI, one that stays useful even when the tools change.

Think of it like driving. Memorizing the buttons of one car is a trick. Learning traffic rules, judgment, and how to stay safe lets you drive any car.

**What is “AI Fluency”**?

The lesson defines it as the ability to work with AI in a way that is:

**Effective**: you get good results.
**Efficient**: you don’t waste time or effort.
**Ethical**: you use it fairly and honestly.
**Safe**: you avoid harm and risk.

An example is learning a new language. Fluent means you can actually use it well in real situations, not just know a few words.

**The focus**: collaboration

The course is about working together with AI, not just understanding how AI is built. Picture a new teammate who is very fast and very knowledgeable but sometimes confidently wrong. You need to know how to give them work, how to check what they hand back, and when to trust them.

The 4Ds (just names for now)

The course is built around four skills, called the 4Ds. The lesson only names them. The next lessons explain each one.

Skill	Simple meaning
**Delegation**	Deciding what to give to AI and what to do yourself
**Description**	Explaining clearly what you want
**Discernment**	Checking whether the AI’s answer is actually good
**Diligence**	Using AI responsibly and taking ownership of the result

A quick memory trick: **Decide, Describe, Double-check, Do it responsibly**.

**What you’ll have by the end**
A framework for thinking clearly about AI
Confidence in choosing when and how to use AI
Practical collaboration skills
The ability to check and share AI-assisted work responsibly
