**Why do we need AI Fluency**?

AI is now part of everyday work, and it keeps changing quickly. Being fluent means you have more than a few tricks. You have:

**Skills**: you know how to use it
**Knowledge**: you understand what it can and can’t do
**Insight**: you have good judgment about when to use it
**Values**: you use it honestly and responsibly

The goal is the same as before: working with AI in a way that is effective, efficient, ethical, and safe.

**Think of a calculator**. Someone who knows only which buttons to press can get stuck. Someone who understands numbers knows when the answer looks wrong. AI Fluency is that second kind of person.

**Three ways people work with AI**

The core of the lesson is that people use AI in three different ways. I’ve used examples from your sales world, since the lesson itself didn’t include any.

1. **Automation**: “Do this specific task.”
You give clear instructions, and the AI does the job. You’re the boss, and it’s the helper following orders.
Example: “Rewrite this follow-up email to sound more polite and keep it under 100 words.”

2. **Augmentation**: “Let’s work on this together.”
You and the AI act as thinking partners, going back and forth. Neither of you does it all alone.
Example: You’re stuck on how to handle a client who says “your price is too high.” You and Claude brainstorm responses, you pick the best ones, Claude suggests improvements, and you refine them together.

3. **Agency**: “Learn how I work, then act for me.”
Instead of giving one task at a time, you set the AI up in advance with its knowledge and its way of behaving. Then it works on its own for you.
Example: You set up an AI assistant with your company’s pricing, tone of voice, and common customer questions, so it can handle customer queries without you instructing it each time.

A simple way to remember
Mode	Who leads?	Like...
**Automation**	You give orders	Telling a worker exactly what to do
**Augmentation**	You and AI together	Brainstorming with a teammate
**Agency**	AI works on its own, within the rules you set	Training an employee, then trusting them

The more independence you give the AI, the more important it is to set it up carefully and check its work.

**Quick revision notes**
**AI Fluency** = skills, knowledge, insight, and values for working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely.
It matters because AI is everywhere and keeps changing.
**Automation**: AI completes tasks from your instructions.
**Augmentation**: you and AI think and work as partners.
**Agency**: you configure AI to act independently for you.

**Link to AI Security**

Agency is where security gets serious. When AI acts on its own, with access to tools, files, or emails, a mistake or an attack can cause real damage without a human in the loop. A lot of AI security work focuses on exactly this: keeping autonomous AI safe and under control. Keep that idea in mind as you go.
