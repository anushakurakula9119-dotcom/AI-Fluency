**Lesson 3: The 4D Framework**

**Course**: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (Claude Academy) Lesson length: 45 min (video is 5 min, plus exercises)

**Learning goal**

Explain the basic AI Fluency Framework and its core 4Ds: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.

1. Recap: **what is AI Fluency**?

Engaging with AI in ways that are effective, efficient, ethical, and safe.

2. Recap: **three ways we engage with AI**
Mode	Meaning	Simple analogy
**Automation**	AI executes specific tasks based on your instructions	Giving a worker exact orders
**Augmentation**	You and AI collaborate as creative thinking and task-execution partners	Brainstorming with a teammate
**Agency**	You guide AI to work independently on your behalf, shaping its knowledge and behavior rather than specific actions	Training an employee, then trusting them

4. **The 4Ds** (the core of this lesson)
**D**	One-line definition	Plain-English meaning	**Question to ask yourself**
**Delegation**	Deciding what work to do with AI vs. yourself **|||**	Choosing the right job for the right worker	"Should AI do this, should I, or should we split it?"
**Description**	Communicating effectively with AI systems **|||**	Explaining clearly what you want	"Have I given enough context, tone, and goals?"
**Discernment**	Evaluating AI outputs critically	**|||** Checking the answer before trusting it	"Is this correct, useful, and what I asked for?"
**Diligence**	Ensuring responsible AI collaboration **|||**	Taking ownership and using AI honestly and safely	"Am I being transparent and responsible with this?"

**Memory trick**: Decide, Describe, Double-check, Do it responsibly.

4. **Key points to remember**
The 4Ds are four core competencies (skills) of AI Fluency.
They apply across all three modes (Automation, Augmentation, Agency). They are not tied to one way of using AI.
They work together, not as separate steps you do once.
They are built to be lasting skills. Developing them prepares you for whatever AI evolution brings next, because they do not depend on any one tool.

6. **Exercise from the lesson** (pick one scenario)
   
**Communication project**: drafting marketing campaign emails with AI

**Delegation**: What do you handle yourself vs. collaborate on with AI?
**Description**: How do you communicate tone, purpose, and success criteria?
**Discernment**: What criteria show the drafts meet your needs?
**Diligence**: What transparency and responsibility considerations matter?

**Research project**: analyzing a large dataset for a research paper

Delegation: How do you divide the analysis between you and AI?
Description: What context does AI need about your research question?
Discernment: How do you verify the AI's analysis for accuracy?
Diligence: What ethical issues arise when publishing AI-assisted research?

**Creative project**: developing character concepts for a story

Delegation: Which creative elements do you explore with AI vs. develop alone?
Description: How do you guide AI to fit your story's world?
Discernment: How do you decide what to keep, modify, or discard?
Diligence: How do you acknowledge AI's contribution?

8. **Reflection questions** (answer in your own words)
   
Which of the 4Ds am I most confident in? Which needs development?
Can I recall a recent AI interaction where the framework would have helped?
Which 4D skills would most improve my work or personal projects?

My own examples (sales / resume-writing context)

Scenario: using AI to write follow-up emails to leads.

**Delegation**: AI drafts the first version. I decide who to contact and what offer to make.
**Description**: I give the lead's situation, my tone (friendly, not pushy), and the goal (book a call).
**Discernment**: I check facts, names, and prices, and whether it sounds like a real person.
**Diligence**: I never send claims I haven't verified, and I protect customer data.

Link to AI Security (my goal)
Discernment = never blindly trust AI output (hallucinations, manipulated output).
Diligence = responsible, safe use (data privacy, accountability).
Agency = highest risk, because AI acts on its own with less human checking.
