##Lesson 10: A Closer Look at Discernment

Course: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (Claude Academy) Section: **Discernment** (third of the 4Ds) Lesson length: 20 min

Note: the lesson page gives a short video summary (no transcript). The analogies and examples below are my own, in simple words.

**Learning goals**
Evaluate AI outputs and processes thoughtfully.
Develop critical thinking skills for AI interactions.
Identify and address quality concerns in AI interactions.

1. **What is Discernment**?

Discernment = **thoughtfully evaluating what AI produces, how it produces it, and how it behaves**.

It is the flip side of Description.
**Description** = communicating my intentions clearly.
**Discernment** = judging whether what I got back meets my needs.
Together they keep AI collaboration guided by human judgment.

**Analogy**: a teacher marking homework. It's not enough to see an answer. You check whether it's right, how the student reached it, and how they behaved while working.

2. The three types of Discernment 
**Type**	                        **What I evaluate**	                                   **Questions to ask**	                                                                                                               **Analogy** (marking an exam)
**Product Discernment**	     The quality of the output	                      Is it accurate? Appropriate? Coherent? Relevant? Any factual errors?                               	                                 Is the final answer correct?
**Process Discernment**	     How the AI reached the output	                   Does the reasoning follow logically? Any gaps, logical errors, or inappropriate reasoning? Are concepts connected properly?	      Did the student show correct working?
**Performance Discernment**	   How the AI behaved in the interaction	           Did it stay on my question and respond to feedback? Is the style and terminology suitable? Does the tone help clarity?	        Did the student listen and communicate well?

Memory trick: Product = **the answer**, Process = **the reasoning**, Performance = **the behavior**. (Same three words as Description, now used for checking.)

3. **Key points**
Description and Discernment form a continuous feedback loop. Each informs the other (covered in the next lesson).
Even the most advanced AI benefits from human judgment and oversight.
Domain knowledge makes Discernment stronger. The more I know about a topic, the better I can spot errors.
Discernment applies to output, reasoning, and behavior, not just the final answer.


**Quick revision**
Discernment = evaluating AI's output, reasoning, and behavior.
Three types: Product (the answer), Process (the reasoning), Performance (the behavior).
Description + Discernment = a continuous feedback loop.
Domain knowledge improves Discernment.
Even advanced AI needs human oversight.


**Link to AI Security** (my goal)
Never trust AI output blindly: hallucinations and manipulated outputs are real risks.
Process Discernment matters for security: wrong or tampered reasoning can lead to unsafe actions.
Performance Discernment helps notice when an AI behaves unexpectedly, which can be a sign of prompt injection or jailbreak attempts.
Human review is a core safeguard, especially when AI can take actions (Agency).
