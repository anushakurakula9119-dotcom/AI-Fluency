Lesson 11: **The Description-Discernment Loop**

Course: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (Claude Academy) Section: The Description-Discernment loop Lesson length: 10 min

Note: the lesson page is mostly an exercise (no video transcript). The analogies and examples below are my own, in simple words. The page refers to the project plan from an earlier lesson (it calls it "Lesson 5"; in my notes that is Lesson 7: Project Planning and Delegation).

**Learning goals**
Apply Description and Discernment skills to a real project.
Engage in productive Description-Discernment feedback loops.
Create results through human-AI collaboration that exceed what either could achieve alone.

1. **The big idea**
Description (**telling the AI what I want**) and Discernment (**judging what I got**) work together in a repeating cycle.

**Analogy**: a potter shaping clay. Shape it, look at it, adjust, look again. Nobody makes a perfect pot in one touch.

2. **The loop: four steps**
   
**Step	                               What I do	                                                                                                    Uses**
1. Describe  	        Say what I need: the output (Product), how to approach it (Process), how to behave with me (Performance)	                 Description
2. Discern	         Evaluate the response: the output (Product), the approach (Process), the behavior (Performance)	                            Discernment
3. Refine            	Give feedback on what worked and what didn't, adjust my description, ask for improvements until satisfied	                     Both
4. Integrate	           Add my own expertise and creativity; decide what to keep, modify, or discard; take responsibility for the final output	   Human judgment

**Memory trick**: Describe, Discern, Refine, Integrate.

Repeat the loop for each task until the project is complete.

3. **The two sets of "3 Ps" side by side**
	                     **Description (before)**	                       **Discernment (after)**
Product	             What output do I want?	                    Is the output accurate and useful?
Process	             How should the AI approach it?	                Did the AI reason well?
Performance	          How should the AI behave?	                    Did it behave the way I needed?


**Quick revision**
The Description-Discernment loop = Describe, Discern, Refine, Integrate.
Each loop uses Product, Process, Performance on both sides.
Repeat per task until the project is done.
Final step is human: add expertise, choose what to keep, and take responsibility.
Goal: results better than either human or AI could produce alone.

**Link to AI Security (my goal)**
The loop is a human-in-the-loop control: the AI never has the last word.
Refine and Integrate are where errors, hallucinations, and manipulated outputs get caught before they cause harm.
Taking responsibility for the final output is the bridge to the next lesson (Diligence).
Repeated testing and refining is the same habit used in red teaming.
