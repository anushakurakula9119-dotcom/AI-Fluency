##Lesson 6: A Closer Look at Delegation

Course: AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations (Claude Academy) 

Section: **Delegation** (first of the 4Ds) Lesson length: 6 min video, plus exercise

**Learning goals**
Understand the Delegation competency and its three components: **Problem Awareness, Platform Awareness, Task Delegation**.

Recognize when and how to delegate tasks to AI effectively.

Build awareness of task, platform, and mode considerations when working with AI.

**Note**: the lesson page gives a short video summary (no transcript). The analogies and examples below are my own, in simple words.

1. **What is Delegation**?

Delegation = **thoughtfully deciding what work you do alone, what you do with AI, what AI does independently, and how the work** is split.

**Analogy**: a class group project. Before starting, the team decides who researches, who writes, who designs, and who checks the final work. Delegation is that planning step, with AI as one of the team members.

2. The three components
Component	                        Meaning	              Simple question	                                 Analogy
**Problem Awareness**	    Understand your goals and the work needed to reach them	"What am I actually trying to achieve, and what does good look like?"	Knowing the exam topic before you study
**Platform Awareness**	Know what different AI systems can and can't do	"Is this AI the right tool for this job?"	Knowing which tool to use: a hammer vs. a screwdriver
**Task Delegation**	Split the work between you and AI to use each one's strengths	"Which pieces go to AI, which stay with me, which do we do together?"	Giving each teammate the part they're best at

Memory trick: **Problem (what), Platform (with what), Task (who does which part)**.

3. Key points
Delegation happens before you start working with AI.
Clarify goals and the nature of the work before involving AI.
Effective delegation needs both:
**Domain expertise** (you know your field)
**AI capability knowledge** (you know what AI can and can't do)

The goal is not to automate everything. **It is to build the most effective human-AI partnership for each task or goal**.

Consider the mode too (Automation, Augmentation, Agency): how independent should the AI be?

5. **How the earlier lessons connect**
**Capabilities & limitations (Lesson 5) feed Platform Awareness**: knowing what AI can do and where it fails helps you choose what to delegate.
Tasks needing **judgment, creativity, ethics, or accuracy checks** usually stay with the human.

7. Exercise: build a delegation plan with Claude
Pick a small task (draft an email, outline a presentation, plan a meeting or event).
Tell Claude the task and ask for help making a delegation plan (what to give AI, what to keep).
Discuss these together (back-and-forth, not one-sided):
What is the overall vision, and what does a good result look like?
What separate pieces of work are needed?
Which pieces need human expertise, creativity, or judgment?
Write a simple delegation plan combining your strengths with the AI's.
Sample plan (my own example: follow-up emails to leads)
**Piece of work**	                                        **Who does it**	                                           **Why**
Decide who to contact and what offer to make	              Me	                                      Needs business judgment
Write the first draft	                                     AI	                                       Fast, good at language
Adjust tone to fit the lead	                             Me + AI together	                           Needs my knowledge of the customer
Check facts, names, prices	                                Me	                                         AI can hallucinate
Send the email	                                            Me	                                               I'm accountable

**Quick revision**
Delegation = deciding who does what: me, AI, or both.
Three parts: Problem Awareness, Platform Awareness, Task Delegation.
You need domain expertise + AI knowledge.
The goal is the best partnership, not automating everything.
Plan the task before opening the chat.


**Link to AI Security (my goal)**
Over-delegating (letting AI act with too little oversight) is a major security risk, especially in Agency mode.
Platform Awareness means knowing a tool's limits and risks (data privacy, hallucinations, access to files and tools).
Deciding what NOT to give AI (e.g. confidential customer data) is a basic security habit.
